## 🧩 Problem & Validation (Q1–Q10)

**Q1. Why this problem? Why now?**  
👉 “It’s growing fast due to [current trend]. If we don’t solve it now, the impact will worsen.”

**Q2. Who exactly faces this problem?**  
👉 “Targeted: [specific group, e.g., small businesses in rural areas].”

**Q3. How did you validate it’s real?**  
👉 “We spoke with 20 users and found the same pain point repeated.”

**Q4. Isn’t this already solved?**  
👉 “Yes, but existing solutions are costly/complex. Ours is simple + affordable.”

**Q5. Why did you pick this problem statement?**  
👉 “It matches both our domain knowledge and tech strengths.”

**Q6. How critical is this issue?**  
👉 “It impacts 5M users annually, with $2B in losses.”

**Q7. Who will benefit the most?**  
👉 “Primary users: students. Secondary: institutions using our data.”

**Q8. How common is this problem?**  
👉 “Our survey showed 80% of respondents struggle with this regularly.”

**Q9. How is this problem being solved today?**  
👉 “Through manual work or fragmented apps. We unify the process.”

**Q10. What’s at stake if this isn’t solved?**  
👉 “Time, money, and opportunities are being lost daily.”

## 🚀 Solution & Product Fit (Q11–Q20)

**Q11. What exactly is your solution?**  
👉 “A mobile app that [core benefit in 1 line].”

**Q12. What’s your USP?**  
👉 “Offline support + regional language = wider adoption.”

**Q13. What’s innovative here?**  
👉 “70% automation vs. manual work in existing tools.”

**Q14. Show us your demo.**  
👉 “Login → upload → instant output in <10 seconds.”

**Q15. Is this feasible in real life?**  
👉 “Yes, because we used scalable APIs + modular design.”

**Q16. What stage is your product in?**  
👉 “Working prototype. Core feature live, extras simulated.”

**Q17. How will you take this beyond hackathon?**  
👉 “3-month roadmap: pilot → refine → launch v1.”

**Q18. How did you test usability?**  
👉 “Shared with 10 users, fixed 3 key UX issues they pointed out.”

**Q19. What makes your approach different?**  
👉 “We design bottom-up for users, not top-down features.”

**Q20. How do you know this works?**  
👉 “Because we’ve tested with real users, not just theory.”

## ⚙️ Technical Feasibility (Q21–Q30)

**Q21. What stack are you using? Why?**  
👉 “React + Firebase = fast build, scalable, widely supported.”

**Q22. Why didn’t you use X tech?**  
👉 “Time limits. But we’ve mapped migration if needed.”

**Q23. Is this working live or simulated?**  
👉 “Core flow live, AI predictions simulated but planned.”

**Q24. How will it scale?**  
👉 “Cloud hosting + modular APIs = scale-ready.”

**Q25. Can this handle 10k users at once?**  
👉 “Not yet, but architecture supports adding load balancing.”

**Q26. How secure is this?**  
👉 “Encrypted data + minimal collection + role-based access.”

**Q27. Did you train your AI model?**  
👉 “No, used prebuilt API to save time, but trainable later.”

**Q28. What if the AI fails?**  
👉 “Core app still works. AI is an add-on, not backbone.”

**Q29. Can this integrate with govt/industry platforms?**  
👉 “Yes, modular API design makes integration easy.”

**Q30. Show your system architecture.**  
👉 “3-layer: frontend → backend → database. Lightweight + modular.”

## 📊 Market & Users (Q31–Q40)

**Q31. Who is your primary user?**  
👉 “College grads preparing for jobs.”

**Q32. Why would they care?**  
👉 “Saves them time + increases success rate.”

**Q33. How big is the market?**  
👉 “10M users. Even 1% = 100k potential adoption.”

**Q34. Who are your competitors?**  
👉 “LinkedIn + XYZ. But they don’t address local needs.”

**Q35. How will you reach users?**  
👉 “Campus drives + WhatsApp groups + early adopters.”

**Q36. What adoption do you expect in 6 months?**  
👉 “Target: 1,000 pilot users → refine → scale.”

**Q37. What’s your edge over competitors?**  
👉 “Simple, low-cost, community-trusted.”

**Q38. How will you build trust?**  
👉 “Transparency in data + testimonials from early adopters.”

**Q39. Who influences your users’ decisions?**  
👉 “Faculty + peers. We’ll leverage both.”

**Q40. How do you keep users engaged?**  
👉 “Gamification + streaks + feedback loops.”

## 🌀 Tricky & brain stuck Questions (Q51–Q60)

**Q51. Why your team? Why not others?**  
👉 “We have lived the problem + the skills to solve it. Rare combo.”

**Q52. What if nobody wants this?**  
👉 “We’ll validate with 20 real users in 2 weeks, pivot if needed.”

**Q53. What if a competitor launches tomorrow?**  
👉 “We’ll win with speed, niche focus, and community trust.”

**Q54. What’s your Plan B if this fails?**  
👉 “Pivot to an adjacent problem with same learnings.”

**Q55. How will you execute after hackathon?**  
👉 “Roadmap: prototype → pilot → refine → incubator.”

**Q56. Did you use version control?**  
👉 “Yes, GitHub repo with commit history + team contributions.”

**Q57. What was your biggest technical bottleneck?**  
👉 “API limits. Solved via caching + simplified queries.”

**Q58. Which part of the solution did each member build?**  
👉 “Frontend: X, Backend: Y, Design: Z, Research: A.”

**Q59. What if regulations block you?**  
👉 “We’re aware. Backup plan = adapt to existing gov frameworks.”

**Q60. Why should we care about your solution?**  
👉 “Because it impacts lives directly. It’s not just an app; it’s a solution to [core pain].”
